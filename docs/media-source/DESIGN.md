# FestivalFit — title sequence

## Style Prompt
Extend the existing FestivalFit product identity into an editorial film-title graphic. Use the product's warm cream, deep plum and lime, its serif/sans pairing, and a ticket-like fictional research dossier. The mood is an independent cinema programme: confident, warm, precise and quietly animated. The first readable frame should communicate the product without waiting for the animation. Motion is a slow projector sweep, a moving research indicator and restrained entrances.

## Colors
- `#2d263b` — plum canvas and ink, from the product's `--ink`.
- `#f6f5f0` — warm paper and primary light text, from `--paper`.
- `#d7ef86` — lime emphasis, from `--lime`.
- `#eee8f4` — lavender paper accents, from `--lavender`.
- `#705488` — purple secondary labels on light backgrounds, from `--purple`.

## Typography
Georgia for expressive display statements and film titles. Arial for the existing FestivalFit wordmark, interface-like labels and technical attribution. Large type, generous line height, and tabular digits for the film runtime. This preserves the product's human story / precise research distinction.

## Motion
One 10-second scene. Entrances finish during the first 1.6 seconds; the GIF uses the fully readable 1.6–9.6 second interval. A research indicator travels across the bottom. Ticket rows receive a restrained sequential tint. A faint projector ring drifts behind the ticket. All motion is deterministic and finite. No narration.

## What NOT to Do
- No neon gradients, glowing technology grids, stock film footage or fake awards.
- No invented accuracy, adoption or winning claims.
- No unreadable miniature interface replicas in the hero.
- No flashing, shaking, rapid cuts or motion-dependent copy.
- Label the illustrated dossier as fictional sample data.
